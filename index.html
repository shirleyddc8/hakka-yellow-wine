OUR MISSION

“To bring ancestral warmth to the modern table by sharing pure liquid gold - honouring ancient wellness traditions and preserving Hakka heritage, one small batch at a time. ”

Our Core Values

  • Cultural Preservation: Crafting every batch by hand to ensure a beautiful piece of our Chinese culture isn't lost to time in modern Singapore.
